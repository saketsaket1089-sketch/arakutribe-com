Araku Tribe Website

Official Website: [https://arakutribe.com/](https://arakutribe.com/)

Araku Tribe is an e-commerce platform inspired by the rich coffee heritage of Araku Valley, Andhra Pradesh. The platform showcases Araku Valley coffee along with branded coffee cups, mugs, and T-shirts, bringing together traditional coffee culture and a modern online shopping experience.

About the Project

The Araku Tribe website provides customers and business partners with a platform to explore coffee products, browse collections, discover special offers, and place bulk orders.

The website focuses on delivering a smooth shopping experience while highlighting the heritage, quality, and sustainability associated with Araku Valley coffee.

Key Features

- Product Catalogue:** Browse coffee bags, coffee cups, mugs, and T-shirts.
- Product Categories:** Explore products through dedicated collections.
- Product Search & Filters:** Find products using available search, sorting, and filtering options.
- Shopping Cart:** Add products and manage shopping selections.
- Product Details:** View individual product information, pricing, and availability.
- Best Sellers:** Discover popular products.
- Special Offers:** Explore discounted products and promotional collections.
- New Arrivals:** Browse recently added products.
- Gift Combos:** Explore coffee and merchandise combinations.
- Araku AI Agent:** Interactive shopping assistant that helps customers select products, specify quantities, provide delivery details, and proceed through express checkout.
- Order Management:** Access order-related options, including tracking and cancellation.
- Business & Franchise Enquiries:** Information for cafés, retailers, and potential business partners.
- Responsive Interface:** Designed to provide a convenient browsing experience across devices.
